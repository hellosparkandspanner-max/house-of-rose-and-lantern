OUR STORY

Some moments are too special to be hidden beneath an ordinary coat.

It began with a little girl who didn't want to cover up her princess dress.

Her outfit was part of the magic, but the weather still called for something warm. Like so many parents, I wanted her to be comfortable without hiding the very thing she'd been so excited to wear.

That's why House of Rose & Lantern was born: to create beautifully considered children's cloaks that offer warmth while letting those special outfits shine.
